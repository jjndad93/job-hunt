How to run it

Your weekly routine

  1. Every weekday, 30–45 minutes: open the Indeed links above, apply to 2 jobs, and log each one here right away.
  2. Apply in person too. For stores and grocery, walk in on a weekday afternoon, around 2–4pm when it's quieter. Dress neat, bring a printed resume, and ask for the hiring manager by name if you can.
  3. Follow up after 5 days. Anything marked Applied for 5+ days turns orange here. Call or stop by: “Hi, I applied for the ___ position last week and wanted to check on it.” Then hit “Followed up”.
  4. Sunday review, 10 minutes. Look at what got callbacks and do more of that kind of job next week.
  5. Keep your phone ready. Answer unknown numbers politely and set up a simple voicemail with your name. Recruiters often call only once.

Interview answers to practice

“Why do you want to leave fast food?”
“I've learned a lot about customer service and working fast. Now I want to grow somewhere I can build a career.” Stay positive and don't trash your old job.
“You don't have experience in this.”
“I've worked two years in a busy job, I show up on time, and I learn quickly. I trained new people at my last job.”
“What's your availability?”
Know your exact days and hours before the interview. Open availability gets hired fastest.
Ask them one question
“What does a great first month look like in this role?” It shows you care about doing well.

Skip any job that…

  • Asks you to pay for training, equipment or a background check up front.
  • Wants you to cash a check, buy gift cards, or forward packages from home.
  • Asks for your Social Security number or bank info before an interview or offer.
  • Only talks over text or chat apps and never gives a real company address.
